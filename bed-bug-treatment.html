Know the Signs

How to Tell If You Have Bed Bugs

Caught early, bed bugs are easier and cheaper to eliminate. Watch for these tell-tale signs.

Bites in a line

Itchy red bites in rows or clusters, often on the arms, neck, or legs after sleeping.

Blood spots

Small rust- or blood-coloured stains on sheets, pillowcases, or the mattress.

Dark fecal specks

Tiny black dots (like marker spots) along mattress seams, baseboards, and headboards.

Shed skins & eggs

Pale shed skins and tiny white eggs tucked into cracks, seams, and crevices.

A musty odour

A sweet, musty smell in the room can point to a larger, established population.

Live bugs in seams

Flat, apple-seed-sized brown bugs hiding in mattress seams and furniture joints.

FAQ

Bed Bug Questions, Answered

Do I have to throw out my mattress or furniture?

Almost never. Our treatment is designed to eliminate bed bugs in place — including inside mattresses, box springs, and furniture seams — so you can keep your belongings.

Is bed bug treatment confidential?

Yes. We arrive in unmarked vehicles and keep your service completely discreet and private.

How many visits does it take?

Most cases are resolved with a main treatment plus a follow-up visit about two weeks later to confirm full elimination and treat any survivors.

Is it safe for my children and pets?

Yes. We use low-toxic, biological and heat-assisted methods. We simply ask that children and pets stay out of treated rooms for a few hours, then ventilate.

How do I prepare for the visit?

Wash and bag bedding on high heat, vacuum, pull furniture from the walls, and avoid moving items between rooms. See our full bed bug prep guide — we also send a checklist when you book.
