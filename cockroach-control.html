Stop the cycle at the source

Cockroaches hide in warm, humid spots — behind appliances, under sinks, and inside cabinets — and a few quickly become many. Worse, they contaminate food and surfaces and are a leading trigger for allergies and asthma.

Store-bought sprays scatter roaches and rarely reach the nest. Our professional gel-bait approach is carried back to the harbourage by the roaches themselves, wiping out the colony — including the ones you never see.

  • Targets the colony — not just the roaches on the surface.
  • Health-first — reduces allergens and contamination fast.
  • Flexible — one-time treatment or an ongoing protection plan.
Know the Signs

Signs of a Cockroach Problem

Roaches are nocturnal, so the clues usually show up before you see the bugs themselves.

Droppings

Specks like ground coffee or pepper near appliances, in drawers, and pantry corners.

A musty odour

An oily, musty smell that lingers in cupboards usually means an established population.

Egg casings

Small brown capsules (oothecae) glued into cracks mean roaches are breeding.

Smear marks

Irregular brown smears along walls and floors in damp, well-travelled areas.

Daytime sightings

Seeing roaches in daylight often means hiding spots are overcrowded.

Shed skins

As roaches grow they shed skins — finding them points to active breeding nearby.

FAQ

Cockroach Questions, Answered

Why do I see more cockroaches right after treatment?

It's normal — and a good sign. The bait draws roaches out of hiding before it works through the colony. Don't spray store-bought insecticide: it repels them from the bait and undoes the treatment.

Are cockroaches a health risk?

Yes. They can contaminate food and surfaces and are a common trigger for allergies and asthma, especially in children — which is why fast treatment matters.

Is one cockroach treatment enough?

For smaller infestations, a single powerful gel-bait treatment is often enough. Larger or recurring problems do best on an ongoing plan with monitoring.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We use targeted, low-toxic gel baits placed in cracks and crevices out of reach — not blanket sprays. Keep children and pets clear of treated areas for a few hours.

How do I keep cockroaches from coming back?

Keep surfaces clean and grease-free, fix leaks, store food in sealed containers, and seal entry points. We seal and advise as part of the service. See our cockroach signs guide for more.
