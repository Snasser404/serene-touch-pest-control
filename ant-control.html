Treat the colony, not just the trail

The ants you see foraging are a tiny fraction of a colony that can number in the thousands, hidden in walls, under floors, or outdoors. Spraying the visible ants does nothing to the nest — and can even cause some species to split into new colonies.

We identify the species, place bait the workers carry back to the queen, and treat the perimeter — then seal the cracks and gaps they use to get in. For carpenter ants, which tunnel through wood and can damage your home, we use specialized targeted treatment.

  • Species-specific — the right method for pavement, pharaoh, or carpenter ants.
  • Carpenter ant protection — stop wood damage before it spreads.
  • Sealed for good — entry points closed so ants stay out.
Know the Signs

Signs of an Ant Problem

A few ants can mean a large colony nearby. Watch for these signs — especially in spring.

Ant trails

Steady lines of ants along counters, baseboards, and walls toward food or water.

Nests & mounds

Small soil mounds outdoors, or nests behind walls, under floors, and in damp wood.

Winged swarmers

Flying ants indoors usually mean a mature colony is nesting in or near your home.

Wood shavings

Small piles of sawdust-like material are a red flag for destructive carpenter ants.

Ants near food

Activity around the pantry, pet bowls, and sweet or greasy spills points to foraging.

Rustling in walls

Faint rustling inside walls or trim can signal a large carpenter ant nest.

FAQ

Ant Questions, Answered

Why use bait instead of spraying ants?

Spraying only kills the ants you see and can cause the colony to split and spread. Bait is carried back to the nest by the ants, eliminating the queen and colony at the source.

Are carpenter ants dangerous to my home?

Carpenter ants tunnel through wood to nest and can cause structural damage over time. They need prompt, specialized treatment — not just surface spraying.

Will the ants come back?

Once the colony is eliminated and entry points are sealed, ants are very unlikely to return. Our protection plans add seasonal prevention for ongoing peace of mind.

Is ant treatment safe for children and pets?

Yes. We use low-toxic baits and targeted applications placed where pests travel, not broad sprays. Keep kids and pets off treated areas for a few hours.

Why did so many ants appear suddenly?

Ant activity spikes in spring and summer as colonies forage, and a brief surge after baiting is a sign the treatment is working. See our seasonal pest guide.
