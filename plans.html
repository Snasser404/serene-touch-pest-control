What to expect

How your treatment works

Pests have life cycles — our timing is built around them, so the problem actually stays gone.

  1. Day 0

    Inspection & first treatment

    We find where pests live and travel, then treat at the source with pet-safe products.

  2. Days 1–14

    Settle-in period

    The treatment works through the colony. A brief uptick in activity is normal — that means it's working.

  3. ~2 weeks

    Free follow-up visit

    We return to confirm it's gone and re-treat anything remaining — at no extra cost.

  4. Every 3 months

    Seasonal protection (on a plan)

    We come back each season to stop the next wave before it starts — spring ants, summer wasps, fall rodents.

Why a plan pays off

Prevention costs less than an infestation

One untreated problem can turn into a costly one — carpenter ants in the structure, a rodent nest in the walls, a roach population that doubles in weeks. In the GTA, pest pressure shifts with the seasons, so a single spring treatment won't stop the mice looking for warmth in the fall.

A quarterly plan keeps a protective barrier in place all year, catches problems early, and means you never pay for an emergency call again — which is exactly why it's how most of our customers choose to work with us.

Good to know

Plan & pricing FAQ

What's the difference between a one-time visit and a plan?

A one-time treatment fixes a current problem and includes a free two-week follow-up. A protection plan keeps pests out year-round with seasonal visits and free re-treatments between them. Because most pests return with the seasons, a plan is usually the better value.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Our protection plans have no long-term lock-in — you can pause or cancel anytime. We aim to earn your business at every visit.

What does the Quarterly Protection plan cover?

Common GTA household pests — ants, spiders, cockroaches, rodents, wasps and more — across four seasonal visits a year, with free re-treatments between visits if anything shows up.

Is the two-week follow-up really free?

Yes. Many pests like cockroaches and ants need a second pass to break the breeding cycle, so a follow-up visit is included with one-time treatments at no extra charge.

How much will it cost?

It depends on your property, the pest, and the severity — a condo or apartment is different from a detached home, and a light problem is different from a heavy infestation. That's why we don't post fixed prices: we assess your place first (free, no obligation) and give you an exact, all-in quote up front — with no surprises.

Are the treatments safe for my family and pets?

Yes — eco-friendly, low-toxic products applied where pests travel, not broad sprays. We give you simple re-entry guidance after each visit.
